# Florida vs Texas — Critical Differences

Florida (lien_type: "lien"): Tax Certificate, earns statutory interest
(interest_rate), 5% guaranteed minimum however early it redeems.
24-month window from certificate issue date. If unredeemed: certificate
holder applies for tax deed, property goes to public auction — buyer does
NOT automatically get the property.

Texas (lien_type: "redeemable_deed"): Redeemable Deed, NOT interest-
bearing. Flat premium instead: 25% year one, 50% year two (homestead/
agricultural/mineral only; 180-day window for other property types, no
escalation). interest_rate shows 0 for TX — expected, not missing data.
If unredeemed: deed holder retains full ownership after the redemption
period — a fundamentally different endgame than FL.
